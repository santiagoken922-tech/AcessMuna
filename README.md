# AccessMuna

**A Community Accessibility Mapping and Barrier Reporting App**

AccessMuna is a mobile application concept designed to help people discover accessibility information about public places and report barriers that may make those places difficult to access.

## Problem Statement

Persons with disabilities and older adults may have difficulty finding reliable information about ramps, step-free entrances, accessible toilets, and other accessibility features before visiting public facilities. AccessMuna aims to make this information easier to discover and provide a way for community members to report accessibility barriers.

## Target Users

- **Persons with disabilities:** People who need accessibility information before visiting public places.
- **Older adults:** People who may benefit from accessible entrances and pathways.
- **Caregivers and family members:** People who help others plan visits to public facilities.
- **Community contributors:** People who can share accessibility information and report barriers.

## Core Features

1. **Explore Places** — Browse public places and their available accessibility information.
2. **Place Details** — View reported accessibility features of a selected place.
3. **Report a Barrier** — Submit a barrier description, location, and optional photo.
4. **My Reports** — View submitted reports and their statuses.

## Sustainable Development Goal

**SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities**

AccessMuna supports social inclusion by helping users find accessibility information and encouraging communities to identify barriers.
